📱 iOS Shortcuts Setup

🌅 Morning Auto-Open

Have FlowState open automatically every morning at 7am:

  1. Open Shortcuts app on iPhone
  2. Tap Automation tab (bottom)
  3. Tap + (top right)
  4. Choose "Time of Day"
  5. Set time to 7:00 AM
  6. Choose "Daily"
  7. Tap "Add Action"
  8. Search for "Open URL"
  9. Paste your FlowState link
  10. Toggle OFF "Ask Before Running"
  11. Tap Done

🌙 Evening Reminder

Get reminded to log your day at 9pm:

Same steps as above, but set time to 9:00 PM
